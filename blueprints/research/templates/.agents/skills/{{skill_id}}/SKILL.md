# Research for {{idea_name}}

## When to Use

Use this skill when work in this project needs source gathering, evidence evaluation, or a written synthesis for: {{idea_description}}

## Procedure

1. Read `project-plan.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `idea-brief.md`; confirm the question, audience, scope, and success criteria.
2. Find current and relevant sources, favoring primary or authoritative material when available.
3. Record each source's link, date, relevance, and supported claims.
4. Compare evidence, note disagreements and limitations, and separate facts from interpretation.
5. Write a concise answer with citations, uncertainty, and remaining gaps.
6. For Kanban work, save durable findings in the shared project folder and report completed evidence, unresolved gaps, and any next handoff to the planner.

## Pitfalls

- Do not invent sources, quotes, dates, or citations.
- Do not treat search snippets or a single source as sufficient evidence for a consequential claim.
- Flag outdated evidence, conflicts of interest, and unresolved disagreements.

## Verification

- Each material factual claim has a supporting source.
- Sources are relevant and dates are clear where recency matters.
- Conclusions match the evidence, and uncertainty and gaps are explicit.
